GOY FOUNDATION
CYBERSECURITY POLICY
Document Title: Cybersecurity Policy
Organisation: GOY Foundation
Prepared by: Junior Cybersecurity Officer
Version: 1.0
Date: October 2026
1. Introduction
GOY Foundation relies on computers, mobile devices, networks, software, email, cloud services, and digital information to carry out its activities. Protecting these resources from cyber threats is important to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the organisation's information.
This Cybersecurity Policy establishes rules and procedures for protecting GOY Foundation's information systems, devices, networks, employees, and data against unauthorised access, cyberattacks, data loss, malware, phishing, and other security threats.
2. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to:
Protect GOY Foundation's information and digital resources.
Prevent unauthorised access to systems and data.
Reduce the risk of cyberattacks and data breaches.
Establish secure practices for employees and users.
Provide guidelines for responding to cybersecurity incidents.
Protect the privacy and confidentiality of organisational and personal information.
Promote cybersecurity awareness among staff.
3. Scope
This policy applies to:
All GOY Foundation employees.
Volunteers, interns, contractors, and consultants.
Management and administrators.
Anyone authorised to access GOY Foundation's systems or information.
All organisation-owned computers, smartphones, tablets, servers, networks, and other devices.
Cloud services, email accounts, applications, databases, and digital files used for organisational activities.
4. Information Security Principles
GOY Foundation will follow three major information security principles:
Confidentiality
Information should only be accessible to authorised individuals.
Integrity
Information must be protected from unauthorised modification, deletion, or manipulation.
Availability
Authorised users should be able to access information and systems when needed for legitimate organisational activities.
5. Password Security Policy
All users must follow these password requirements:
Passwords must be strong and difficult to guess.
Users should avoid using names, birthdays, phone numbers, or common words as passwords.
Passwords must not be shared with other people.
Different passwords should be used for important accounts.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) should be enabled where available.
Users must report suspected compromised passwords immediately.
Passwords must not be stored in publicly accessible documents.
6. Access Control
Access to GOY Foundation's systems must be based on job responsibilities.
Employees should only have access to information required for their duties.
User accounts must have unique usernames.
Accounts belonging to employees who leave the organisation must be disabled promptly.
Administrative privileges must only be given to authorised personnel.
Users must not attempt to access systems or information without permission.
Access rights should be reviewed periodically.
7. Email and Phishing Security
Employees must be careful when receiving emails, messages, or links from unknown sources.
Users should:
Verify suspicious emails before clicking links or opening attachments.
Avoid providing passwords or confidential information through suspicious messages.
Check the sender's email address carefully.
Report suspected phishing attempts to the appropriate IT or cybersecurity personnel.
Never install unknown software received through email or messaging platforms.
8. Device Security
All devices used for organisational activities must be protected.
Users must:
Use passwords, PINs, or biometric security on devices.
Keep operating systems and applications updated.
Install security updates promptly.
Use approved antivirus or endpoint protection where applicable.
Lock devices when leaving them unattended.
Avoid connecting unknown USB devices to organisational computers.
Report lost or stolen devices immediately.
9. Network Security
GOY Foundation's network must be protected against unauthorised access.
Security measures should include:
Firewalls.
Secure Wi-Fi configurations.
Strong Wi-Fi passwords.
Regular software and firmware updates.
Network monitoring where appropriate.
Secure remote access.
Separation of sensitive systems where practical.
Employees should avoid accessing sensitive organisational information through unsecured public Wi-Fi unless an approved secure connection is used.
10. Data Protection
GOY Foundation must protect confidential and sensitive information.
Users must:
Store organisational information only in approved locations.
Avoid sharing confidential information with unauthorised persons.
Use secure methods when transferring sensitive information.
Protect personal information belonging to employees, beneficiaries, partners, and other stakeholders.
Dispose of sensitive information securely when it is no longer required.
11. Backup Policy
Important organisational information should be backed up regularly.
Backups should:
Be performed according to the organisation's operational needs.
Be protected from unauthorised access.
Be tested periodically to ensure that information can be restored.
Be protected against accidental deletion and ransomware where practical.
12. Malware Protection
GOY Foundation will take reasonable measures to prevent malware infections.
Users must not:
Download software from untrusted websites.
Install unauthorised applications on organisational devices.
Disable security software without permission.
Open suspicious attachments.
Connect unknown storage devices to organisational systems.
Any suspected malware infection must be reported immediately.
13. Cybersecurity Incident Response
A cybersecurity incident may include:
Unauthorised access.
Data theft or loss.
Malware infection.
Phishing attacks.
Ransomware.
Lost or stolen devices.
Compromised accounts.
Unauthorised disclosure of confidential information.
When an incident occurs, users should:
Report the incident immediately.
Disconnect an affected device from the network if instructed or if necessary to contain the threat.
Avoid deleting evidence or investigating beyond their authority.
Follow instructions from the designated cybersecurity or IT personnel.
Document relevant information about the incident.
The organisation should investigate the incident, contain the threat, recover affected systems, and take steps to prevent similar incidents from happening again.
14. Employee Cybersecurity Awareness
GOY Foundation should provide regular cybersecurity awareness training to employees and other relevant users.
Training should cover:
Password security.
Phishing awareness.
Social engineering.
Safe internet usage.
Data protection.
Malware prevention.
Incident reporting.
Secure use of organisational devices.
15. Social Media and Internet Usage
Employees should use social media responsibly when representing or discussing GOY Foundation.
Employees must not:
Share confidential organisational information publicly.
Publish sensitive information about beneficiaries or employees without authorisation.
Use organisational accounts for unauthorised activities.
Click suspicious links or download untrusted content using organisational devices.
16. Remote Work Security
Employees working remotely must maintain appropriate security practices.
They should:
Use secure internet connections.
Protect organisational devices from unauthorised users.
Avoid using shared computers for confidential work.
Lock devices when unattended.
Use approved cloud services and communication platforms.
Report security incidents immediately.
17. Third-Party Security
Third-party vendors, contractors, and service providers who have access to GOY Foundation's systems or information should follow appropriate security requirements.
Where appropriate, third parties should:
Protect organisational information.
Restrict access to authorised personnel.
Report security incidents.
Follow contractual security requirements.
Return or securely delete organisational information when their services end.
18. Physical Security
Physical access to computers, networking equipment, servers, documents, and other sensitive resources must be controlled.
GOY Foundation should:
Restrict access to areas containing sensitive equipment.
Keep important equipment in secure locations.
Protect devices from theft or damage.
Ensure visitors are appropriately supervised where necessary.
19. Policy Violations
Failure to comply with this cybersecurity policy may expose GOY Foundation to security risks.
Depending on the nature and seriousness of a violation, appropriate disciplinary or corrective action may be taken in accordance with organisational procedures and applicable law.
20. Policy Review
This cybersecurity policy should be reviewed periodically and whenever there are significant changes to:
Technology.
Cybersecurity threats.
Organisational operations.
Legal or regulatory requirements.
Security incidents.
Updates should be approved by the appropriate management authority.
21. Conclusion
Cybersecurity is the responsibility of everyone at GOY Foundation. By following this policy, employees and other authorised users can help protect the organisation's information, systems, resources, and stakeholders from cyber threats.
GOY Foundation is committed to maintaining a secure digital environment through prevention, awareness, responsible technology use, incident response, and continuous improvement.
